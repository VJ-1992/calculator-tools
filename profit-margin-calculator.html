Professional Profit Analysis

This calculator is optimized for retail shopkeepers and wholesalers who deal with multiple units and bundle pricing. Understanding your per-piece profit versus your packet profit is crucial for inventory strategy.

Advanced Modes Explained

  • Packet / Bundle Mode: Used when you sell items in fixed counts (e.g., a box of 12 pens). Input the packet price, and the tool finds the per-piece revenue for you.
  • Bulk Sale Mode: Used to calculate the total profit for a specific transaction size. Helpful for generating quotes for customers buying multiple units.

The Math of Margin

Unit Profit = Selling Price (per piece) - Cost Price (per piece)
Margin % = (Unit Profit / Selling Price per piece) × 100
Packet Profit = Unit Profit × Pieces per Packet

Example Scenario

"I buy a box of chocolates for $10 (containing 20 bars). I sell each box for $15."

  1. Cost per Piece: $10 / 20 = $0.50
  2. Selling Price per Packet: $15.00
  3. Selling Price per Piece: $15 / 20 = $0.75
  4. Profit per Piece: $0.75 - $0.50 = $0.25
  5. Margin: ($0.25 / $0.75) × 100 = 33.33%
