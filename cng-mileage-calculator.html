Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my CNG mileage drop in winter?

CNG is stored as a compressed gas. Temperature changes affect gas density and pressure, which can slightly influence the amount of fuel delivered and recorded during filling.

Is it better to fill CNG at night?

Gas is denser when it's cooler. Filling up during cooler parts of the day like night or early morning might allow a slightly more complete fill-up in some scenarios.

How can I increase my CNG car mileage?

Regular servicing of the CNG kit, cleaning the air filter, maintaining correct tire pressure, and smooth gear transitions are the best ways to maximize mileage.
