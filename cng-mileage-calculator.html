CNG Mileage Formula Explained

CNG mileage is calculated by dividing the total distance traveled by the amount of CNG consumed. To find running cost, the CNG price per kg is divided by the mileage in km/kg. This gives an accurate per-kilometer cost for CNG vehicles.

Mileage (km/kg) = Total Distance ÷ CNG Consumed

Running Cost (₹/km) = CNG Price per kg ÷ Mileage

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good CNG mileage in India?

A good CNG mileage for most cars in India ranges between 20 to 30 km/kg depending on driving conditions, car model, and maintenance.

Is CNG cheaper than petrol per km?

Yes, CNG generally has a significantly lower cost per kilometer compared to petrol, often saving owners up to 50-60% on fuel bills.

Does this calculator work for Indian CNG prices?

Yes, it is designed for Indian fuel prices and usage patterns, using the standard km/kg metrics.

Can I calculate monthly CNG cost using this tool?
