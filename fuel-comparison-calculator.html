Formula Used

The calculator determines the total ownership cost using a simple logic: Purchase Price + (Total Kilometers driven over the ownership period / Mileage) * Price of Fuel. For Electric Vehicles, it uses electricity units (kWh) and efficiency (km/kWh) instead of litres or kilograms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an EV always cheaper than a Petrol car?

Not always. While running costs are lower for EVs, the higher initial purchase price means you need to drive a significant distance over several years to "break even" compared to a cheaper petrol vehicle.

Does this include maintenance costs?

This specific calculator focuses on fuel and purchase costs. Maintenance is generally lower for EVs and higher for Diesel cars, which should be considered separately for a full assessment.

How accurate are these comparisons?

These results are estimates based on the inputs provided. Real-world mileage can vary depending on driving conditions, traffic, and maintenance habits.
