Privacy Policy

At QuickCalc Pro, the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This privacy policy document outlines the types of personal information received and collected by QuickCalc Pro and how it is used.

Log Files

Like many other Web sites, QuickCalc Pro makes use of log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, type of browser, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends, administer the site, track user’s movement around the site, and gather demographic information.

Cookies and Web Beacons

QuickCalc Pro does use cookies to store information about visitors preferences, record user-specific information on which pages the user access or visit, customize Web page content based on visitors browser type or other information that the visitor sends via their browser.

Google AdSense and DART Cookie

  • Google, as a third party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on QuickCalc Pro.
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visit to QuickCalc Pro and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy at the following URL – https://www.google.com/privacy_ads.html

Data for Calculations

All numeric inputs used for financial calculations (Principal, Cost, Prices, etc.) are processed locally in your browser and are NOT collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.
