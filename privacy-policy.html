Privacy Policy

At QuickCalcPro, we take your privacy seriously. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data while you use our free online calculators.

Information Collection

We do not require users to create accounts or provide personal details like names or addresses to use our calculators. Any numbers or data you enter into our tools are processed locally on your own device. We do not store or see the specific calculations you perform.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most websites, we use "cookies" to improve your experience. These are small files stored on your computer that help us understand how you use the site and remember your preferences (like currency settings). You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Google AdSense and Third-Party Ads

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements on our website. To provide ads that are relevant to you, Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie). This allows Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to QuickCalcPro and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad Settings page. Other third-party ad networks may also use cookies to measure ad effectiveness; you can manage these through your browser's privacy settings.

User Consent

By using QuickCalcPro, you consent to our privacy policy and agree to its terms. We aim to keep our practices honest and transparent to ensure you feel safe using our resources.
