Formula Used

Simple Interest = (Principal * Rate * Time) / 100. Compound Interest = Principal * (1 + Rate/Frequency)^(Frequency * Time) - Principal. Compound interest is "interest on interest."

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between Simple and Compound interest?

Simple interest is calculated only on the principal amount. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus any accumulated interest from previous periods, leading to faster growth.

How does compounding frequency affect my returns?

The more frequently interest is compounded (e.g., Monthly vs. Yearly), the higher your total return will be, because interest starts earning more interest sooner.

Can I use this for loan calculations?

Yes, you can use Simple Interest mode for basic personal loans or the Compound mode for understanding how interest builds up on credit card debt or long-term business loans.
