How is Simple Interest Calculated?

Calculating simple interest is a basic financial skill used to determine the cost of borrowing or the profit from an investment. Our tool uses standard mathematical formulas to provide accurate results:

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100
Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

In these formulas, Principal is the initial amount of money, Rate is the annual interest percentage, and Time is the number of years. Our simple interest calculator online handles all these variables automatically.

Step-by-Step Calculation Example

Example: Calculating interest for an investment.

  • Principal: ₹10,000
  • Rate: 5% per year
  • Time: 2 years

1. Calculate Simple Interest: (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000
2. Calculate Total Amount: 10,000 + 1,000 = ₹11,000
3. Your Total Earnings: ₹1,000

Frequently Asked Questions

What is simple interest?

Simple interest is the interest calculated only on the original principal amount of a loan or deposit. It does not take into account any interest accumulated over time, making it easier to predict than compound interest.

How is simple interest different from compound interest?

The primary difference is that simple interest is calculated only on the principal, whereas compound interest is calculated on the principal plus the interest that has already been added. Compound interest grows faster over time.

How to calculate simple interest manually?

To calculate it manually, multiply the principal amount by the interest rate and the time period, then divide the result by 100. This is expressed as the simple interest formula: SI = (P × R × T) / 100.
