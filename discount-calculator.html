How to Use the Discount Calculator

Our discount calculator is designed to provide a comprehensive breakdown of your savings. Follow these steps to get an accurate final price:

  • Step 1: Enter the Original Price of the item. This is the price before any discounts are applied.
  • Step 2: Input your Primary Discount. You can toggle between a percentage (%) or a flat amount (Fixed Value).
  • Step 3: If you have a secondary coupon (e.g., "Take an extra 10% off the sale price"), click "Add sequential discount".
  • Step 4: Check the "Apply Sales Tax" box if you need to calculate the total including GST or VAT. Enter the tax rate applicable in your region.

Formula Explanation

The math behind a discount calculation depends on whether the discount is a single stage or sequential. For a standard single discount, we use:

Savings Amount = Original Price × (Discount Rate / 100)
Final Price = Original Price - Savings Amount

If you apply a sequential discount, the second percentage is calculated from the already discounted price, not the original price. This is common in outlet stores or holiday sales.

Example Calculation

Imagine you are buying a jacket listed at $100.

  1. There is a 20% off store sale. Savings = $20. New Price = $80.
  2. You have an extra 10% coupon. This 10% is taken from the $80 price. Savings = $8.
  3. The final price before tax would be $72.
  4. If the sales tax is 5%, the tax would be $3.60, making the final payable total $75.60.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 20% + 10% discount the same as 30%?

No. A sequential discount (20% then an extra 10%) results in a 28% total discount because the second discount is applied to the reduced price, not the original sticker price.

Does the calculator handle sales tax?

Yes, you can enable the tax toggle to add a percentage-based tax to the final discounted price, simulating the real checkout experience at a store.

Can I calculate fixed dollar amount discounts?

Yes. Use the toggle next to the primary discount field to switch from percentage mode to "Flat" mode to subtract a specific currency amount.
