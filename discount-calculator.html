How to Calculate a Discount?

Finding the final price involves subtracting the discount amount from the original price.

Discount Amount = (Original Price × Discount %) ÷ 100
Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example Calculation

If an item is ₹2,000 with a 25% discount, you save ₹500 and pay ₹1,500.
