Professional GST Calculation Guide

Our GST calculator is designed to handle all domestic and international trade scenarios for Indian businesses. Understanding which mode to use is key for accurate accounting.

1. Add GST (Exclusive to Inclusive)

Use this when you know the base price and want to find the final bill amount. Standard for generating sales invoices.

GST Amount = (Base Price × GST Rate) / 100
Total Price = Base Price + GST Amount

2. Remove & Reverse GST (Inclusive to Exclusive)

Use this when you have a total amount (like a MRP) and need to extract the tax portion to find the original value.

Excluding Amount = (Including Price × 100) / (100 + GST Rate)
GST Portion = Including Price − Excluding Amount

Tax Splits (CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST)

  • Intra-State: Split 50:50 between CGST (Central) and SGST (State).
  • Inter-State: Full 100% applied as IGST (Integrated Tax).
  • Union Territory: Split 50:50 between CGST and UTGST.
  • Imports: Treated as Inter-State supplies, attracting IGST (Import).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Remove GST and Reverse GST?

Mathematically, they are identical. Both extract the tax from an inclusive price. "Remove GST" is commonly used by shoppers to find pre-tax prices, while "Reverse GST" is often used by accountants to verify tax entries.

Is IGST applicable on imports?

Yes, under the GST regime, imports into India are treated as inter-state supplies and are subject to IGST in addition to standard customs duties.
