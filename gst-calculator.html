Formula Used

To Add GST: Amount * (Rate / 100). To Remove GST: Amount - (Amount * (100 / (100 + Rate))). For Intra-state deals, the tax is split exactly 50/50 between Central (CGST) and State (SGST).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST is the Central portion and SGST is the State portion of GST for local sales. IGST is a single tax collected by the center for sales between different states or imports.

What is "Reverse GST" calculation?

This is used when you have the final MRP (inclusive of tax) and you want to work backward to find out how much the original item cost before the government added the GST percentage.

Which GST rate should I apply?

In India, essential goods are usually 5%, most services and products are at 18%, and luxury or 'sin' goods are at 28%. Consult your CA for specific item classifications.
