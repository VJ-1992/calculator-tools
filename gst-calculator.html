GST Components in India

Depending on whether goods are sold within a state or across borders, different GST components are applicable. Our calculator automatically determines the correct breakdown based on your selection:

1. Intra-State (Within Same State)

Calculates CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST). Each component is exactly 50% of the total GST rate.

2. Inter-State (Between Two States)

Calculates IGST (Integrated GST). The full tax rate is applied as Integrated Tax to the Central Government.

3. Union Territory (Within a UT)

Calculates CGST and UTGST (Union Territory GST). This is similar to intra-state but replaces SGST with UTGST for territories like Chandigarh or Andaman and Nicobar.

Formula Guide

Add GST: Final Price = Base × (1 + Rate/100)
Remove GST: Base Price = Total / (1 + Rate/100)
Splits: CGST/SGST/UTGST = Total GST / 2

Frequently Asked Questions

When is IGST applicable?

IGST is applicable on all Inter-state supplies of goods and services. For example, if a seller in Maharashtra sells to a buyer in Delhi, IGST will be charged.

Is CGST always half of the total GST?

Yes, in Intra-state and Union Territory transactions, the Central Government takes 50% of the GST collected, which is represented as CGST.

Does this tool support UTGST?

Absolutely. By selecting the "Union Terr." option, the tool will correctly display the UTGST component alongside CGST.
