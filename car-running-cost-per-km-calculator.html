Running Cost Per KM Formula Explained

Car running cost per kilometer is calculated by dividing the fuel or electricity price by the vehicle’s mileage. This gives an accurate estimate of how much you spend to drive one kilometer. The formula is as follows:

Cost per KM (₹) = Fuel Price ÷ Mileage (km per unit)

Total Monthly Cost = Cost per KM × Monthly Distance

Frequently Asked Questions

Which car has the lowest running cost per km in India?

Electric (EV) and CNG cars usually have the lowest running cost per kilometer in India, often ranging between ₹1.00 to ₹3.00 depending on driving style and city utility rates.

Is diesel cheaper than petrol per km?

Historically, yes. Diesel cars often have a lower cost per km due to better thermal efficiency (higher mileage), though the gap in fuel prices has narrowed in recent years.

Does this calculator work for Indian fuel prices?

Yes, this tool is designed specifically for Indian fuel metrics (Litre, KG) and electricity slabs (kWh), using the Rupee (₹) symbol.

Can I calculate monthly car fuel cost?

Yes, by enabling the "Calculate Monthly & Yearly Cost" toggle and entering your average monthly travel distance.
